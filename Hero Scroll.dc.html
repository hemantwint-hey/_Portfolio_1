Said Out Loud.

Some thoughts were easier to write than to speak.

A calmer
mind.
A kinder me.
“Nature is perfect.
It made you.”

I Cannot Explain

How can I not say
that nature is perfect,
when it created a life like you?

Your eyes—
like a flowing stream,
quiet and still,
yet somehow sweet enough
to make the world forget its noise.

I never said I was a good man.
I carry lust,
anger,
pain—
things I don't always know how to silence.

But then you smile.

And somehow,
everything inside me disappears,
like those feelings
were never mine to begin with.

I don't know
if it's only my imagination,
or if there is something
I cannot put into words.

Maybe it's just a moment.
Maybe it's nothing.

But when I look at you,
for a little while,
the world feels right.

And I don't know why.

I just know
I cannot explain it.

— Hemant
